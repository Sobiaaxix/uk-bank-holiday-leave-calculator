Do Bank Holidays Count as Annual Leave?

The short answer: it depends on your employment contract. There is no automatic right to take bank holidays off work in the UK.

All UK workers are entitled to 5.6 weeks (28 days) of paid annual leave per year under the Working Time Regulations 1998. Your employer can structure this in two ways:

Bank Holidays Included

Your 28-day entitlement includes bank holidays. If there are 8 bank holidays, you have 20 days left to book freely.

Bank Holidays on Top

You receive 28 days plus all bank holidays on top — giving you up to 38 days total in Northern Ireland.

Part-Time Workers

Entitlement is calculated pro-rata. A 3-day week gives 16.8 days annual leave per year.

Zero-Hours Contracts

You still accrue holiday pay at 12.07% of earnings if you have no fixed hours.
