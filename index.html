Do Bank Holidays Count as Annual Leave?

It depends on your employment contract. All UK workers are entitled to 5.6 weeks paid leave per year under the Working Time Regulations 1998. Employers can include bank holidays within this allowance or give them on top.

For a full breakdown by nation and contract type, visit ukholidaysinfo.uk.
