# UK Bank Holiday Leave Calculator

A reference tool for UK bank holiday dates and annual leave 
entitlement across England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

## About

UK bank holidays vary by nation. England and Wales observe 8 bank 
holidays in 2026. Scotland observes 9 and Northern Ireland observes 10.

This repository provides a quick reference for developers building 
leave calculators, payroll systems or HR tools that need accurate 
UK bank holiday data.

## Do Bank Holidays Count as Annual Leave?

This is one of the most common questions UK workers ask. The answer 
depends entirely on your employment contract.

All UK workers are entitled to 5.6 weeks paid annual leave per year 
under the Working Time Regulations 1998. Your employer can include 
bank holidays within this allowance or give them on top — both are 
legal.

For a full breakdown by nation and contract type, 
[learn more here](https://ukholidaysinfo.uk/do-bank-holidays-count-as-annual-leave-in-the-uk/).
